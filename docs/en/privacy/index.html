Privacy policy

Your ideas, creations, and sharing choices are yours.

Effective and updated: November 6, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the Voxamber App and this website and is provided by the Voxamber developer. In the current import-first workflow, you obtain scene code in your own AI service and choose to import it for generation, preview, editing, and export on iPhone. The App is in development testing. We will update this page as actual data-handling practices change.

2. Content kept on your device

Original imports, scene source, generated models, drafts, saved revisions, and local run records are stored on your device. System sharing saves only the text or file you confirm to the local inbox. Voxamber does not upload this code or work to our servers. No Voxamber account is required, and the App contains no advertising or third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. Third-party AI you choose

Voxamber does not require AI model downloads, collect or manage third-party AI accounts, passwords or API keys, or send AI conversations on your behalf. You submit descriptions or code, manage accounts, and pay for services directly in the third-party App you choose. Its settings, terms and privacy policy apply; that processing is separate from local compilation in Voxamber.

4. Camera, files, and system sharing

The system requests camera access only for features you choose, such as AR preview; this does not require sending camera imagery to us. File import reads the content you select. Sharing from another App requires your confirmation to save, and Voxamber does not fetch AI conversation links. You choose model and source export destinations. File and cloud providers apply their own policies.

5. Content you choose to send

You can take scene code back to your own AI for revision, or export models and source to Files, cloud storage, or another App. The receiving service handles those copies under its policy. If you submit GitHub Issues or TestFlight feedback, that platform processes the content and account information you provide. GitHub issues may be public: do not include sensitive information.

6. Diagnostics and Apple services

The App may keep compilation timing and other run records locally for troubleshooting; it does not automatically upload them to our backend. Apple may process diagnostics and feedback according to your system settings, TestFlight use, and its policies. Those platform activities are separate from third-party analytics SDKs embedded in Voxamber.

7. Retention, data management, and backups

Original imports, source, drafts, and saved revisions remain on the device. Acknowledging a shared item does not delete creations. Export anything you need before using data-management options in your installed version or removing the App through iOS. We hold no remote creative-content account or server copy. Manage system backups, exports, AI conversations, and feedback separately in the appropriate service.

8. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or tracking cookies set by us. GitHub may process access logs and other information to deliver and protect its hosting service under its privacy statement. Language pages are switched by links without tracking storage.

9. Children and content

Voxamber is a creative tool and has no child-specific data collection flow. Only import, execute, and share scene code you have the right to use. Code may be incompatible and results incorrect or incomplete; inspect them before use, modification, or publication.

10. Contact and changes

For privacy questions, data-management concerns, or policy suggestions, use the GitHub contact entry on our support page. Describe the issue first and do not submit personal information in a public issue. We will update the date on this page when the policy changes and provide notices when required.
