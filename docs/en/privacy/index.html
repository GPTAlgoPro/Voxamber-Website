Privacy policy

Your ideas, creations, and sharing choices are yours.

Effective and updated: November 5, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the Voxamber App and this website and is provided by the Voxamber developer. The App is currently in development testing. We will update this page as actual data-handling practices change.

2. Content kept on your device

Design descriptions, project parameters, generated models, drafts, and local run records are stored in the App’s storage on your device. We do not operate a cloud inference service that receives these creative materials. The App does not require an account and contains no advertising or third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. Model resources

Initial installation or updates of model resources require a network connection. Model packs are being prepared for Apple-hosted asset distribution; development builds may also use public Hugging Face or ModelScope resources. Resource providers process network information needed for delivery and security, such as IP addresses, resource requests, and connection times. A resource download does not upload your descriptions or creations as part of the download. Each provider’s own policy applies.

4. Camera and files

The camera is used only when you choose features that need it, such as system AR preview, and the system requests permission. This use does not require uploading camera imagery to us. The file picker grants access to resources you choose; you control export and share destinations.

5. Content you choose to share

You can export creations to Files, cloud storage, or another App. Those copies are governed by the service you choose and its policy. If you submit feedback through GitHub Issues or TestFlight, the platform processes your submission and account information. GitHub issues may be public: do not include sensitive information.

6. Diagnostics and Apple services

The App may keep run records such as generation timing locally for inspection and troubleshooting. It does not automatically upload them to our own backend. Apple may collect or provide diagnostics and feedback to the developer according to your system settings, TestFlight use, and Apple’s policies. This platform processing is separate from third-party analytics SDKs inside the App.

7. Retention, deletion, and backups

You can delete projects, remove model resources, or remove the App using iOS. We have no remote creative-content account or server copy to delete centrally. Manage system backups, exported copies, and feedback in the relevant system or service. Removing model resources and deleting creations are separate operations.

8. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or tracking cookies set by us. GitHub may process access logs and other information to deliver and protect its hosting service under its privacy statement. Language pages are switched by links without tracking storage.

9. Children and content

Voxamber is a creative tool and has no child-specific data collection flow. Only enter and share materials you have the right to use. Generated results may be incorrect or incomplete; inspect them before using, modifying, or publishing them.

10. Contact and changes

For privacy questions, data-management concerns, or policy suggestions, use the GitHub contact entry on our support page. Describe the issue first and do not submit personal information in a public issue. We will update the date on this page when the policy changes and provide notices when required.
