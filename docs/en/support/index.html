Before you begin

On-device AI requires a supported iPhone with at least 12GB of RAM and a complete set of model resources. Initial downloads need a network connection and sufficient storage. Follow the requirements shown in the App.

Model downloads and offline use

Complete the model download and integrity check in the On-device AI screen before using AI. Retry an incomplete or invalid download, or use the import option provided in the App. Resource packs and App versions are updated separately.

A generation fails or the App closes

Record the App version, iPhone model, iOS version, approximate time, and steps to reproduce. Check Creations for your previously saved work. Failures may occur during development testing; visible progress alone does not mean saving has completed.

Saving, exporting, and AR

Check Creations after generation completes. Export GLB or USDZ using the system share sheet and choose a destination. External storage and syncing are handled by the service you select. AR preview requires camera permission and device support.

Managing local data

Delete work in Creations and remove model resources from model management. Removing a model does not remove exported copies. Removing the App deletes its local data according to iOS behavior; manage system backups and external copies separately.

Beta feedback

Invited TestFlight testers can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight. Apple processes feedback and crash diagnostics under its platform rules. No public download or testing-invitation link is currently offered here.

Useful details for a report

  • App version and build number
  • Device model and iOS version
  • Steps and expected result
  • The message you saw or approximate time
